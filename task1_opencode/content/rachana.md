## What I think about AI in software engineering today
AI makes development faster and helps explore solutions that might not come to mind immediately. However, it still needs human judgment to ensure the solutions are correct, reliable, and actually solve the problem. I see it as a tool to support developers, not replace their understanding.

## Where software engineering will be in 5–10 years
We'll probably spend less time writing code from scratch and more time guiding AI, reviewing its work, and fixing what it gets wrong. Knowing how to code will still matter, but knowing how to think through a problem will matter even more.

## My hot take on AI
AI can write a thousand lines of code in seconds, but finding the one tiny mistake that breaks everything is a different story.
